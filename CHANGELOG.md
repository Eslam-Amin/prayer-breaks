## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-19

### Added
- Live status bar countdown to the next obligatory prayer (`Fajr`, `Dhuhr`, `Asr`, `Maghrib`, `Isha`).
- Rich Markdown hover tooltip displaying today's prayer schedule, Arabic names, Hijri date, and quick action links.
- Offline astronomical prayer time calculation powered by `adhan`.
- Automatic IP geolocation on first run with regional calculation method detection, plus 34+ city presets and manual coordinate support.
- Pre-prayer heads-up and on-time prayer notifications with **Start Break** and **Snooze** actions.
- Active prayer break countdown timer in the status bar.
